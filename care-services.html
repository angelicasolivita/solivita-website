Services & Amenities

At Solivita, care is personal. With a maximum of six residents and a 1:3 caregiver-to-resident ratio, our team has the time and attention to provide support that is truly tailored to each person. Every care plan is built around the individual, and every day is designed to support residents in living this next chapter with comfort, dignity, and purpose.

Personalized Care Plans

Every resident's care begins with an assessment to understand their needs, preferences, and routines. We build a care plan that reflects who they are and update it as those needs evolve.

  • Individualized care plan with regular reassessments
  • Consistent care team assigned to each resident
  • Family involvement in care planning

Daily Living Assistance

Our caregivers provide hands-on support with the activities of daily living, delivered with patience, dignity, and respect for independence. We help where help is needed and step back where it's not.

  • Bathing, grooming, and dressing
  • Medication management and reminders
  • Mobility and transfer assistance

Health & Safety

Solivita is a licensed RCFE providing non-medical residential care. We coordinate closely with outside providers to ensure residents receive comprehensive support.

  • 24/7 care with awake overnight staff
  • Coordination with physicians and specialists
  • Hospice approved and home health friendly

Dining

Meals are prepared fresh in our home kitchen three times a day. We accommodate dietary needs and preferences, and shared meals are one of the ways our small community stays connected.

  • Three delicious meals prepared daily
  • Snacks and beverages available throughout the day
  • Dietary accommodations honored

Enrichment & Activities

Life at Solivita is active, social, and full of purpose. Our enrichment programming is planned weekly and designed around the residents themselves.

  • Weekly enrichment programming
  • Seasonal and holiday programming
  • Scheduled transportation for outings and appointments

Housekeeping & Home Services

Solivita is nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in the Otay Lakes community of Southern San Diego. Our home is designed to feel warm, comfortable, and distinctly not institutional.

  • Private and shared rooms available
  • Regular housekeeping, laundry, and trash service
  • WiFi throughout the home
