Care & Services

At Solivita, care is personal. With a maximum of six residents and a 1:3 caregiver-to-resident ratio, our team has the time and attention to provide support that is truly tailored to each person.

Personalized Care Plans

Tailored to each resident's needs, preferences, and routines. Our dedicated caregivers provide consistent, one-on-one support with the same team every day.

  • All-inclusive monthly rate
  • 1:3 caregiver-to-resident ratio
  • Round-the-clock care team, including overnight
  • Choice of solo or shared rooms

Daily Living Assistance

Support with medication management, grooming, dressing, and day-to-day needs, delivered with dignity, patience, and respect for independence.

  • Medication management
  • Hospice approved
  • 3 daily meals, prepared fresh
  • Weekly housekeeping, laundry, and trash service

Enrichment & Activities

Planned weekly programming with scheduled activities, group outings, and social events. Every day at Solivita is active, social, and full of life.

  • Planned weekly enrichment programming
  • Scheduled transportation for outings and appointments
  • Group social events and community outings

Who Solivita Is Right For

Solivita serves ambulatory adults ages 59 and older who benefit from daily companionship, structured support, and personalized care in a safe home environment. Our home is designed for aging in place, providing the support residents need while respecting their independence.

We do not currently accept residents with advanced dementia or who are non-ambulatory.
