Services & Amenities

At Solivita, care is personal. With a maximum of six residents and a 1:3 caregiver-to-resident ratio, our team has the time and attention to provide support that is truly tailored to each person. Every care plan is built around the individual, and every day is designed to support residents in living this next chapter with comfort, dignity, and purpose.

Our Home

Our single story home has an open floorplan and was designed for comfort & accessibility.

  • 2,850 Sq. Ft.
  • 5 Beautifully Designed Bedrooms
  • Maximum 6 Residents
  • Private Sunset-Facing Backyard
  • Private and shared rooms available
  • WiFi throughout the home

Our comprehensive services include:

  • Personalized Care Plans Built around each resident's needs and coordinated with their physician.
  • 24/7 Supervision A 1:3 caregiver-to-resident ratio with awake overnight staff, for peace of mind, always.
  • Daily Living Assistance Dressing, grooming, hygiene, medication management and more.
  • Medication Management Administered by trained caregiving staff, tracked and on schedule.
  • Three Chef-Prepared Meals Daily Nutritious, delicious and home-cooked.
  • Housekeeping & Laundry Regular weekly service to keep the home clean and comfortable.

Additional Support

Our care extends beyond the checklist.

  • Engaging Activities & Outings Social connection and enrichment.
  • Family Owned & Operated Hands-on, personally involved ownership.
  • Care Team The same caregivers get to know each resident's preferences, routines, and needs.
  • Health Coordination Hospice approved, home health friendly, and coordinated with outside physicians and specialists.
  • Family Involvement Regular updates and family involvement in care planning.
