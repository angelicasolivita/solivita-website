**FAQ**

**What type of care does Solivita provide?**

Solivita is a licensed Residential Care Facility for the Elderly (RCFE) in California. We provide non-medical residential care including 24/7 supervision, medication management, daily living assistance, meals, housekeeping, and enrichment activities. We are not a medical facility and we do not providenursing care.

**Who is Solivita right for?**

We serve ambulatory adults ages 59 and older who benefit from daily companionship, structured support, and personalized care plans in a safe home environment. Solivita is designed for aging in place, providing the support residents need while respecting their independence. We do not currently accept residents with advanced dementia or who are non-ambulatory.

**How many residents live at Solivita?**

A maximum of six. Our intentionally small community allows us to provide truly individualized care with a 1:3 caregiver-to-resident ratio that large facilities cannot match.

**What does the monthly cost include?**

Our all-inclusive monthly rate covers personalized care, 24/7 supervision, three chef-prepared meals daily, medication management, housekeeping, laundry, activities, and all comfort amenities. Pricing is based on an initial care needs assessment.

**How do I schedule a tour?**

Click "Book a Tour" anywhere on our site. We'll ask a few quick questions to make sure Solivita is the right fit, then schedule a time for you to visit in person.

**Mission**

At Solivita, we believe that every stage of life deserves to be lived fully, with peace, purpose, and connection.

We bring that belief to life by:

**Providing personalized care in a home designed to feel like a breath of fresh air** for up to six Southern California residents. 

**Dedicating ourselves to the standard of care our founder spent her career enforcing**, where every detail is intentional and nothing is bare-minimum.

**Creating a community where residents are valued, supported, and never alone.**

**Our Story**

Angelica Medina is the founder of Solivita. She spent her career as an Investigator at the Community Care Licensing Investigations Bureau, a law enforcement role where she responded to priority 1 and 2 elder abuse cases across Southern California. She saw what happens when care falls short and knew she could provide the level of care she spent her career enforcing.

Using her eye for detail and her expertise in what it means to provide exceptional care at this stage of life, Angelica created Solivita to be the gold standard of what residential care can look like. A home designed to prove that senior living can be beautiful, dignified, and full of life, where residents feel at home and families feel at ease. Built to the standard she would want for her own loved ones.

The name Solivita means sun and life, chosen to reflect the belief that senior living can be a bright new chapter, full of peace, purpose, and genuine connection.

**Our Values**

**Community:** Aging often means isolation. Through shared meals, daily activities, and genuine connection, we make sure no one here is alone. Because where there is community, every day is full of life.

**Peace:** A calm, restful environment where the pace of life is unhurried and designed to bring comfort. Our home and our team are built to support the daily needs of our residents so they can move through each day with ease. And for families, that same peace extends to knowing that their loved one is cared for, supported, and in good hands every hour of the day.

**Intention:** Built from a deep understanding of what residential care requires and a vision to reimagine what it could be, Solivita is curated down to the smallest detail. Our six coastal-inspired rooms each have their own character, and every element of the home, from the dedicated care team to the enrichment programming designed around the seasons, the community, and the residents themselves, is selected with the same level of thought and intention. Because when you know what this industry looks like at its worst, you pour everything into making it look like its best.

**Purpose:** Life at Solivita is designed around more than care. It's designed around living. Through enrichment programming, daily routines, and community activities, residents are given the space and support to stay engaged, connected, and living with purpose.

**Belonging:** Leaving a home of 50+ years is one of the hardest transitions a person can make. Solivita is designed to make that transition easier by providing a space where residents can thrive in this next chapter of their lives and truly feel at home.
