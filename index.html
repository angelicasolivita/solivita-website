Solivita Assisted Living — A Bright New Chapter of Life

Our Approach

The name Solivita means sun and life, chosen to reflect the belief that senior living can be a bright new chapter. Our founder created Solivita with a clear vision for what senior living should feel like. Intentionally small, with a maximum of six residents, so every person receives the kind of personalized care that larger facilities simply cannot provide.

From the coastal-inspired rooms to the enrichment programming designed around our residents, every part of Solivita is built with the same level of thought and intention as the care.

Care & Services

Personalized Care Plans

Tailored to each resident's needs, preferences, and routines. Our dedicated caregivers provide consistent, one-on-one support with the same team every day, so residents are cared for by people who genuinely know them.

Daily Living Assistance

Support with medication management, grooming, dressing, and day-to-day needs, delivered with dignity, patience, and respect for independence. We follow the standard of care our founder spent her career enforcing.

Enrichment & Activities

Planned weekly programming with scheduled activities, group outings, and social events. Life at Solivita is active, social, and grounded in the belief that there's still so much life to live.

FAQ

What type of care does Solivita provide?

Solivita is a licensed Residential Care Facility for the Elderly (RCFE) in California. We provide non-medical residential care including 24/7 supervision, medication management, daily living assistance, meals, housekeeping, and enrichment activities. We are not a medical facility and we do not provide nursing care.

Who is Solivita right for?

We serve ambulatory adults ages 59 and older who benefit from daily companionship, structured support, and personalized care plans in a safe home environment. Solivita is designed for aging in place, providing the support residents need while respecting their independence. We do not currently accept residents with advanced dementia or who are non-ambulatory.

How many residents live at Solivita?

A maximum of six. Our intentionally small community allows us to provide truly individualized care with a 1:3 caregiver-to-resident ratio that large facilities cannot match.

What does the monthly cost include?

Our all-inclusive monthly rate covers personalized care, 24/7 supervision, three chef-prepared meals daily, medication management, housekeeping, laundry, activities, and all comfort amenities. Pricing is based on an initial care needs assessment.

How do I schedule a tour?

Click "Book a Tour" anywhere on our site. We'll ask a few quick questions to make sure Solivita is the right fit, then schedule a time for you to visit in person.
