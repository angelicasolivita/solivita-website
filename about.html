Our Mission

At Solivita, we believe that every stage of life deserves to be lived fully, with peace, purpose, and connection. Our mission is to bring that belief to life by:

  • Providing personalized care in a home designed to feel like a breath of fresh air for up to six Southern California residents.
  • Dedicating ourselves to the standard of care our founder spent her career enforcing, where every detail is intentional and nothing is bare-minimum.
  • Creating a community where residents are valued, supported, and never alone.

Our Story

Angelica Medina is the founder of Solivita. She spent her career as an Investigator at the Community Care Licensing Investigations Bureau, a law enforcement role where she responded to priority 1 and 2 elder abuse cases across Southern California. She saw what happens when care falls short and knew she could provide the level of care she spent her career enforcing.

Using her eye for detail and her expertise in what it means to provide exceptional care at this stage of life, Angelica created Solivita to be the gold standard of what residential care can look like. A home designed to prove that senior living can be beautiful, dignified, and full of life, where residents feel at home and families feel at ease. Built to the standard she would want for her own loved ones.

Our Values

Community

Aging often means isolation. Through shared meals, daily activities, and genuine connection, we make sure no one here is alone. Because where there is community, every day is full of life.

Peace

A calm, restful environment where the pace of life is unhurried and designed to bring comfort. Our home and our team are built to support the daily needs of our residents so they can move through each day with ease. And for families, that same peace extends to knowing that their loved one is cared for, supported, and in good hands every hour of the day.

Intention

Built from a deep understanding of what residential care requires and a vision to reimagine what it could be, Solivita is curated down to the smallest detail. Our six coastal-inspired rooms each have their own character, and every element of the home, from the dedicated care team to the enrichment programming designed around the seasons, the community, and the residents themselves, is selected with the same level of thought and intention. Because when you know what this industry looks like at its worst, you pour everything into making it look like its best.

Purpose

Life at Solivita is designed around more than care. It's designed around living. Through enrichment programming, daily routines, and community activities, residents are given the space and support to stay engaged, connected, and living with purpose.

Belonging

Leaving a home of 50+ years is one of the hardest transitions a person can make. Solivita is designed to make that transition easier by providing a space where residents can thrive in this next chapter of their lives and truly feel at home.
